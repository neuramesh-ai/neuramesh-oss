00What this round adds

Today the HQ runs one flow: setup → four brand docs → a strategy whose cadences you arm → social drafts → approve → publish. marketing-os catalogs the other thirteen surfaces a working marketer touches — scored site audits, GEO, competitor teardowns, hooks, email, launches, positioning, ASO — as pure-markdown skills (MIT). This round vendors them as a second bundled pack and makes each flow a playbook: a data-registry entry (shared/playbooks.ts, the SETUP_FLOWS idiom) that rex can list and launch (list_playbooks / run_playbook), that runs as a plan-first unit or a chat turn, and that lands a scored report whose score trends run over run. Zero new tables — skills, units, schedules, artifacts and tags carry everything.

Round 2 — where it lives. Room home screens are hidden now; the shell is New-chat-as-home plus workspace destinations. So the marketing surface is a destination: Marketing OS, under SHORTCUTS beside Whiteboards · Scheduled · Files — workspace-wide, narrowed by its own ScopeBar (search · project), exactly the ruling that ungated the Calendar and Files (a surface gated behind one room is structurally unreachable; docs/33 §8). Unscoped it is the marketing floor across every project; scoped it is one project's growth desk. The round-1 room-home strip and catalog popover are retired into it.

04The ReportCard contract — score, basis, gaps; then the trend

One card serves every scored playbook (audit, GEO, teardown, ASO). Anatomy: kicker · the dial · serif title · the basis line (what was actually accessed — the honesty spine on the card face) · dimension bars · fix-first count · the gaps line, never hidden · actions. A re-run wears its delta; the first run wears none. Re-measure arms the suggested cadence through the existing schedule gate — Free's 402 routes to the upgrade card, exactly like arming any routine.
