Phase ring — the task panel's journey, wound into a dial

The panel header carried the phase spectrum as a full-width ribbon of coloured bars (docs/24 §2). At that scale it spent the widest line on the screen to say one thing — you are on leg 3 of 5 — and said it in a language you had to hover to read, directly under the task's own title. This replaces the panel variant with a 30px ring at the title's right edge: one dotted arc per leg, the position in the middle, the whole journey on hover.

Nothing about the data changes. Same derived legs (journeyFor, pure + unit-tested), same state tokens, same rules: done solid · live filled by the working agent's beats and breathing · the road ahead ghosted in its own hue · an unstaffed leg a dashed hollow in the blocked tone. The board card's 4px whisper is untouched.
