Decisions — ALL SEVEN APPROVED by George, 2026-08-28

This round is the locked visual contract for W5. The list below stands as approved; revisions reopen the gate.

1 · The machine dial vocabulary (provisioning / waking / awake / idle) as the one state language everywhere.
2 · The free offer framing: "Free cloud machine with every new workspace" as the announcement pill above the shipped hero (round 2's move, kept in round 3).
3 · The attribution moment: "on your cloud machine ☁ · replied in Ns" on early replies, retiring afterward.
4 · The browser wizard reorders (r4): Workspace · Machine · Keys · Team · Launch — provisioning starts at Workspace-submit, where the id exists; the Machine step is a non-blocking watch-it-come-up beat; the desktop wizard keeps its shipped order. Build note: workspace creation moves from Launch (nm.onboard) to the Workspace step's Continue in the browser flow.
5 · The Keys delta + Starter compute: subscription mode becomes "sign in on your machine after launch" (in-thread terminal), and free users pass the Keys gate on the metered Starter pack — making the shipped hint ("a curated free pack is coming soon") real.
6 · The creed edit: "completely local" → "local or cloud — your choice" — the only line of shipped landing copy this round touches.
7 · Setup follows the first reply, never precedes it; the subscription card leads the asks.
