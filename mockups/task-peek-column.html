Clicking #1016 in a thread reply shouldn't cost you your place. The ask: a mini task view that opens next to the conversation, expandable to the full task thread when you decide to switch to it — while tasks opened from Home, Recents or the nav keep taking the whole surface as they do today.

The good news: this is a second TENANT, not a new surface. docs/33 §8 already etched it — "a surface that must be looked at and talked about at once docks as a resizable peer column beside the thread, never as a position: fixed overlay: a hairline grip, the thread keeping its --col measure, ⤢ expands it to the whole sheet by rotating the same parts. First use: the Design Studio." The machinery is live (.tsplit · .sgrip · one animated --studio-basis). Stop 1 is live — click #1016, drag the seam, press ⤢.

Esc closes the peek; the thread was never covered, so there's nothing to restore.
