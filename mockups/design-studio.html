The Design Studio

Reviewing a design today means leaving the conversation: DesignReview is a position:fixed; inset:0 overlay, so opening a mockup hides the thread, and its one-line feedback box closes the overlay on send and starts a five-minute round. There is nowhere to simply ask.

The studio docks the stage beside the thread instead — resizable, with the round's conversation under it and the human-only gate in reach. You just talk: the composer has no modes to pick. What you said that should change collects as a note, and one Redraw spends them all in a single round.

R2 — the mode chip is gone

R1 put an Ask / Request changes toggle on the composer. It made you classify your own sentence before typing it, and it still burned one round per note. PlanReview already had the answer: accumulate, then spend once.
