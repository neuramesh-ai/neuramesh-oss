Marketing workflow — content tasks get their own trade

The Marketing HQ gave rooms a lens and a toolbelt but kept engineering's task machinery underneath — so a chat ask like "draft two X posts" still becomes a build-style task: a docs kind, a PR-style Definition of Done, Diff/Terminal tabs, a result.md file. This proposal makes the work match the trade. rex gathers a quick brief and hands it to the marketer, who already knows your product, brand, guidelines, connected networks and tools by virtue of the room. Drafts arrive as beautiful, uniquely-identified post cards, inline in the thread — review, revise, or schedule each in place. The board scopes to the room. Two rules hold: reuse everything that fits (the FSM, content_items, the calendar, the approve gate) and diverge only where marketing genuinely differs — no new task states, no parallel pipeline, we bridge two systems that already exist.

no new FSM states — marketing labels are a lens posts = content_items attached to the task marketer context injected at fan-out (stageBrandContext) inline <SocialPostCard> — extracted from PostPreviewModal board scoped to the room
The model — two lifecycles that compose (both already in the code)
THE PIECE · a task, existing FSM relabeled → Ideas→Queued→Drafting→Review→Reviewed→Approved ·  human gate
THE POSTS · content_items, per network → draft→scheduled→published|failed ·  on the cards + calendar

What ships, in order

Each slice is shippable and evidence-gated. Backend before desktop, as always.
1 · Kind-aware triage + brief card. channel.kind into the orchestrator; the marketing PHASE-2 branch; kind:'content' (0089); route-to-marketer; publish-readiness DoD.

3 · Inline <SocialPostCard>. Extract from PostPreviewModal; content.create gains taskId; watchContentByTask; unique ids + per-post actions.
4 · The marketing panel lens. Tabs Thread·Posts·Calendar; marketing facts + gate + spectrum.
5 · Channel-scoped board. Scope + toggle; marketing columns; Queue/Live lens.
