Home & Feed — a clickable prototype

Today a room has two surfaces with two composers that look identical and do different things: Home's says “Describe the work — the team takes it from there” and starts work; Feed's says “Message #channel” and posts a message. Home is the default, so a new room opens on a dashboard rather than its conversation — and openRoom also force-opens the history panel, so arriving can present three surfaces at once. That is the mode error behind “I typed in the feed and nothing happened”.

The proposal: the feed is the room, Home is the workspace. Kill the duplicate per-room Home and the per-room landing pin (roomLandingGet/Set) with it; keep genuine alternate views like marketing's Calendar and Library. Click the ten steps to walk both versions.
