The brain picker, compact — the popup learns its own interior lesson

The problem (live, on the stage): the Roles popup opens ~590px wide with a fixed 372px body — sized for a roles TABLE — over a surface whose whole point is quiet. Most opens are a glance ("what am I on?") or a one-flip (scope, pack); the table is the rare deep edit, yet it pays its full footprint every time.

The insight is already in the component's own doc (docs/10 §15): inside the popup, the model picker takes over the body with a ‹ back row, because a dropdown can't live in a scroller — "one surface, one scroller, one Escape; Escape unwinds exactly one layer." Direction A promotes that exact law one level up: the popup opens SMALL, and Roles / Pack are drill rows into the same fixed-height body. The drill card below is live — click the brain pill, drill in, back out.
