Review in a tab — one surface for every artifact that needs a verdict

The plan review is a full-screen overlay today: it takes the whole window, hides the room, and exists only for implementation plans. v0.71.0 made the content area tabbed, and a review is content. This round moves it into a tab and — the part that matters — makes it a type, not a component per kind: an implementation plan, a release plan, a design round and whatever comes next are the same tab wearing different data.

The claim to test in these frames. A review tab is { artifact, gate, verdict[] }. The artifact supplies the bytes and the language. The gate says who may decide and what the decision is called. The verdict options are the buttons. Nothing below is drawn per kind — the two frames differ only in the data bound to them, which is the whole proposal.

Open questions — answer these and I build it. ① Does approving close the tab? Proposal: yes, and it drops you back on the conversation where the verdict was announced — the tab existed for a decision that no longer needs making. ② Can two review tabs be open at once? Proposal: yes, they are ordinary tabs; the reuse rule already means one artifact is one tab, so you cannot open the same plan twice. ③ Does the old overlay stay for anything? Proposal: no — it is deleted in the same PR. Two doors to one review is the duplication that made the dock worth retiring. ④ Where do comments live while unsent? Proposal: on the tab, so switching away and back keeps a half-written batch — losing review comments to a tab switch would be the worst possible first impression of this change.
