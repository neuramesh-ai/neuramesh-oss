The thesis

NeuraMesh isn't a coding tool — it's the first app where you manage a team instead of typing code. The wow has to come from feeling like a powerful engineering leader commanding a living crew: fast, evidence-gated, and alive. Raw Claude Code is single-threaded and lonely. NeuraMesh should feel like a mission control for your org of one.

The love is won in two places most teams neglect: the first 2 minutes (does the magic land before doubt creeps in?) and the 10,000th minute (is checking your mesh the best part of the morning?).

✅ The palette is already unified

Verified in tokens.css: the website and all four app themes are Ember Weave — ember #ff7a52 on warm-black, #ec5a32 on cream. Color cohesion is done.

So the real opportunity is experiential continuity: the same loop animation, motion, and personas you meet here flow straight into the app's empty states and loading — one feeling, not just one color.
