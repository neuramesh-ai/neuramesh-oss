01 The rail — kill the boxes

What's wrong today: each section (Channels / People / Agents) is wrapped in its own .navgroup — a 1px border at 12px radius. Three outlined cards stack inside a panel that is already a card, so nothing reads as more important than anything else, and the double inset (.navgroup margin 8px + .chan margin 2px) makes rows in different sections sit on different left edges. On top of that, every agent is a two-line row and most of them say "standing by" — eight rows of identical non-news eat ~350px of a 534px rail.

The three directions below share one fix (boxes out, one left edge, one hover-reveal +) and differ on how much the roster costs you.

02 The Agents + — an add-to-room overlay, not a create form

Today App.tsx:12950 wires the Agents + straight to setCreateOpen(true) — the full hire flow. That's the rarest action on the cheapest affordance: adding an existing agent to this room is the common one, and it's currently buried as a hover + that only appears on agents who happen to be visible in the rail.

The swap: + opens a picker scoped to the current channel — search, filters, one-click add — with create parked in the footer where a rarer, heavier action belongs. Both commands already exist server-side (channel.add_agent / channel.remove_agent, handler.ts:1275), and the shell can extend the shipped RosterOverlay (App.tsx:11443) rather than being a new component.

03 A new room — cards to staff it, a papertrail to explain it

Today a fresh channel renders one grey line: No messages in #build yet — say something. (App.tsx:13889). It's a dead end — the room has no agents, no repo, and no way to get either without going back to the rail.

The intro block sits at the top of the feed (it is the beginning of the scroll, so it never covers messages): hero → setup cards → papertrail → first message. Cards fade out per-card as their job is done, so a working room keeps only the hero and the trail.
