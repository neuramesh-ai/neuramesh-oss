The projects rail — build spec

R2 approved (2026-08-01) with three tweaks. All three land, and the second one changes the shape for the better: it turns the sidebar into one column with two faces rather than a strip plus a menu plus a nav.

Tweak 1 · no tile at rest

“I don't think we need the logo of the active project showing in the left rail when collapsed; just name is fine with the pulsing animations as needed.”

Right — the name becomes the identity, so it steps up from --dim to --body, and the strip loses its only non-essential object. Logos are for choosing, which only happens on the open face.

Tweak 2 · the rail becomes the nav

“On reveal, the project rail becomes the project nav instead of a separate nav appearing right of it.”

The strongest note of the round. R2 had a 216px panel beside a 266px nav — two nav-shaped things, one job. Now: one 266px column, two faces, and a 30px spine that switches them.

Tweak 3 · workspace to the rail

“It seems we are moving the profile/workspace icon to that rail? If that's best, it's fine with me.”

Yes — and it pays for itself: with the project named in the spine and the account in its foot, the nav head has nothing left to say. Deleting it returns 56px to Recents.
At restThe spine — a name, two pulses, and nothing else
30px, transparent, --win showing straight through. Top to bottom: Home with its needs-you count (the only filled tile — workspace scope), a hairline rule, a live pulse when an agent is working in this project, the active project's name in vertical mono, one quiet dot per other project (pulsing where work is happening there), and the account tile in the foot. Everything above the rule is the workspace; everything below it, and the whole column to its right, is the named project.

Tweak 2One column, two faces — the money shot
The column never changes width and never moves. Hovering the spine (or ⌘⇧P) swaps its face: rooms out, projects in, a 130ms cross-fade with a 4px slide. Tweak 3What moving the workspace to the spine actually buys
The nav head today is a 38px badge in 9px/11px padding = 56px of column, and it exists to say two things: which project (now the spine's job) and which workspace (now the projects face's). With both re-homed it has no remaining content, so it goes — and the 266px column that docs/32 §12 measured as too tight to hold a full Recents row gets those 56px back. That is two more Recents rows at the current 27px row height, or one row plus the branch line.
