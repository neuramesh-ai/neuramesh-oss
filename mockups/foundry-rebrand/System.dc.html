Foundry System sheet · round 1

What we studied, and what we keep.

The reference is factory.ai. We borrow its typographic system, its neutral ground, and its restraint. The Porch mark, the named crew, and the two themes stay ours.

Observed at factory.ai

Type
Geist for everything. Display in medium, uppercase, tight (about -0.04em, leading 1.0). Section titles in regular weight, sentence case, -0.03em. Geist Mono uppercase for eyebrows and button labels.
Color
Warm-neutral greys: page #f5f5f5, raised #ffffff, sunken #ebebeb. Ink is near black, #020202. A neutral ramp from #d6d3d2 to #1f1d1c. One orange accent, #ef6f2e, used in small doses.
Shape
Controls at 3px, panels at 6 to 12px, 1px borders in the neutral ramp. Primary buttons are dark fills with light labels. Large sunken panels frame a section.
Texture
A grid-paper panel under the product frame with a soft light vignette. Dark cards with grain and a warm glow in one corner.
Motion
Page and hero enter by fade and rise (6 to 14px, 0.32s, ease-out-quint). Product frames push in with a slow scale. Diagonal stripes slide on hover. A caret blinks. Chrome reveals in a stagger.
Layout
A 1440px column with 16px gutters on a 12-column grid. Wide product screenshots in dark chrome. A logo wall for proof. A dark closing card, then a dark footer.

The NeuraMesh answer

Keep
Geist as the one voice, uppercase medium display, mono eyebrows and labels, 3px controls, hairlines, grid paper, the grain card, the fade-and-rise entrance, the hover stripes.
Ours
The Porch mark, oak on cream in every theme. The lowercase neuramesh wordmark. The named crew. Two themes, graphite and paper, both first class.
Retire
The serif display voice (Source Serif and the Instrument italic flourish). Pill buttons on the marketing site. The cream frame gutter. The intro veil.
Levers
Four open decisions ride as tweak chips on every page artboard: theme, accent (ember or oak), paper (neutral or cream), and case (uppercase or sentence).
Proof
No logo wall. We have no customers to show, so the proof strip is the true one: bring your own Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini.
Copy
ASD-STE100 throughout. No em dashes, no semicolons, active voice, one idea per sentence. The locked triad stays the headline.

The accent is for small things: a live dot, a numbered kicker, the stripes under a pressed button, a glow in the closing card. Primary buttons are ink, never the accent. The Porch mark keeps oak on cream in every theme. It never recolors with the accent, even when the accent is oak.
