Failure alerts — the attention bar

When something the human armed stops working — a connector's grant dies, a scheduled post fails, a routine errors — the failure currently lands in a grey chip on a calendar you may not visit, or in a console.error nobody reads. This round gives failures ONE derived surface: a quiet amber bar at the top of the Home stage (it exists only while something is broken), and the same rows pinned as an Attention section in the bell. Every row carries the verbatim reason and the one action that resolves it.

Reading notes. The bar is derived, not stored: it renders from three synced row conditions (connectors.status = 'reauth_required' · schedules.last_error · content_items.status = 'failed') and disappears the moment each condition clears — reconnect succeeds, the routine’s next run lands, the posts re-approve. No dismiss, no ack table, no unread state. Rex never writes the bar; the daemon’s deterministic sweep (the stall watchdog) gains a routine_failing class so rex can additionally triage repeat failures in the room (re-run, or raise an nmq card) — detection is code, action is judgment. Post failures whose reason maps to a dead grant fold INTO the connector row (one cause, one card, the fix on it) rather than listing beside it.
