The rules this obeys

One derived number

fill = needsYou + inFlight. Empty = 0 · partial = 1–3 · full = 4+. Nothing new to configure, and the states can't drift apart.

The mark keeps oak

--brand / --brand-ink are constant in all four themes. Dark surfaces stay achromatic graphite — Porch is the one warm object on them.

Never two display moments

The greeting already speaks in serif. Porch is a picture, so it can share the screen with it — but the giant wordmark can't, which is why its ink stays under ~6%.

Standard cut ≥ 24px

Below that the handoff's small cut takes over (wider arch, r=3.5 eyes, stroke-4 smile). The top bar and the composer cap use it.

Clipped, not floating

The wordmark sinks behind the composer and is cropped by the sheet. Cropping is what turns a logo into texture.
