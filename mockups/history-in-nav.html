History in the left nav — three ways, at the real 266px

The proposal: move each room's History out of its tab and into the left nav under Agents, search at the top, following the active channel — and on Home, spanning every channel. The need behind it is real: today History costs you the conversation (it is a tab, so opening it replaces the Feed), and the rail below Agents is often empty.

The constraint that decides it: .navpanel is 266px, and after padding + the 30px row icon a title has about 190px — roughly 24 characters. Every variant below is drawn at that exact width with real threads from #build, so the truncation is honest rather than argued.

A · The History surface as it is, dropped into the nav
Faithful port: today's .histrow — 30px icon, title, preview line, state chip, timestamp — at 266px. Shown with People and Agents collapsed (the best case, and the state in the report) and expanded (six agents with status lines, the state you work in).
