Rex suggestion pills — the reply predicts your next move

The rule the design encodes: a pill is a pre-drafted message, never a hidden command. Rex ends a chat or thread reply with 2–4 short follow-ups written in your voice; they render as quiet pills under his newest reply, and clicking one simply sends that text as your message through the exact send path you'd have typed it into — same wake, same thread, same audit trail. The moment anything of yours lands after, the pills are gone. Zero schema, zero PowerSync, zero new commands: a fenced nms block in the message body (the nmq twin), parsed and stripped at render.
