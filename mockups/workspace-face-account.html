One workspace column

The ask (George, 2026-08-04): fold the account popup's items into the workspace face that slides in to switch projects — there is a lot of empty space at the bottom, and it may be a better home for them. Then delete the popup.

Worth doing, and the reason is stronger than the empty space. The face already opens with an identity header — avatar + Copper Atelier · workspace. The popup opens with another identity header — avatar + owner + email. Two panels, both anchored on the same avatar, on the same spine, ~40px apart, each answering "where am I / who am I" about the same workspace. That is the duplication; the empty space is just what makes the fix free.

The placement that proves it: "Local · Free — 1 of 3 projects" is a statement about the projects list. In the popup it floats above a Workspace section with no projects in sight. In the face it sits directly under the projects it counts — and it explains, in place, why ＋ New project sometimes opens an upgrade modal.

The hazard that shapes the design: the face opens on HOVER. The popup is click-only today, so Sign out has never been something you could reveal by drifting your pointer at the left edge. A flat dump of every item into a hover panel changes that. Hence zoning, not stacking — see Stop 3.

Stop 4Three directions, and why B
directionshapeverdict
A · flat stack every popup item laid into the face as sections — plan, Workspace, You, prefs — all visible at rest Rejected — puts Sign out on a panel that opens on hover, and turns a switcher you glance at into a menu you read. The switch job is the panel's reason to exist and it would be sharing the frame with seven settings rows.
B · zoned face switch stays on top and untouched · plan strip under the projects it counts · workspace nouns in the dead space · account footer at the bottom, expanding in place Recommended — kills the popup, keeps the switcher instant, puts each item where its subject already is, and keeps destructive actions behind a click. The empty space gets filled by the things that were homeless, not by everything.
C · split by axis workspace items into the face; personal items (profile, theme, sign out) stay in a much smaller avatar popup Viable fallback — strictly better than today and less work than B, but it does not achieve the ask: the popup survives, so the second identity header and the second plane both survive with it.
Stop 5What to decide before this is built
decide

Does hover still open it?

B is safe on a hover panel because nothing destructive shows at rest. But if the footer becomes a place you go often, hover-opening a panel with your email in it may start to feel exposed on a shared screen.

Cheapest answer: leave hover as it is, and watch whether you ever reach for the footer without having clicked the rail first.

decide

Settings vs Workspace settings

The face's header already says workspace, so the row can read Settings rather than Workspace settings — the panel supplies the noun. Shown that way in the mockups.

Against: ⌘K and the tour both name it "Workspace settings", and a renamed row is a renamed thing to search for.
