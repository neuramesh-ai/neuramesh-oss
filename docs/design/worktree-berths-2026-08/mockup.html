Chart method (dataviz skill, applied)

Form: change-over-time composition → stacked area, one axis, no dual scales. Series identity = the new --viz-berths/donors/clones tokens — the SAME identity on the card bar, the area bands, the breakdown dots and meters (color follows the entity across surfaces). Palettes validated per theme on the card surface: lightness band, chroma floor, CVD separation, normal-vision floor, contrast — all pass (validate_palette.js, dark #1e1e1e · light #ffffff). Marks: 2px surface gap between stacked bands, recessive grid (three horizontal hairlines), text in text tokens only. Hover: crosshair + tooltip (date, three values, total), 12ms fade, hit target = full plot column. Legend present (3 series) + the bands are direct-labeled at their right edge.

Status color discipline

--warn appears ONLY on the fleet (third-party sprawl — genuinely a warning, icon+label carried by the row text), never as a fourth series. Reclaim feedback wears --green.
