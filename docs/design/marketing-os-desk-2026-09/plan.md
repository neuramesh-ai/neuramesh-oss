## 2. What was wrong with the page

- Three stacked sections: the project cards, then eleven playbook rows, then the threads. The
  catalog took about 600px, so the threads started below the fold with no door to them.
- Unscoped, every playbook row wore the same `Run for… ›` button, eleven times.
- Every thread row repeated `ROUTINE` and `flowe-ai · #marketing`, and nothing said which rows needed
  the human.
- The desk (score, cadences, connections) was the smallest part of the page.
- The taglines carried em dashes and arrows. CLAUDE.md #11 bans both.

## 3. What was decided

- **The page is today's page.** The desk, then the playbooks, then the threads underneath as you
  scroll. "All" is the page itself. Nothing narrows unless a pill is on.
- **One bar under the desk.** The pills at the LEFT, the two section words at the RIGHT, `PLAYBOOKS
  · THREADS`, one line, in the rail's kicker voice. The lit word is the section under the bar and
  carries its count. The dim word is the door and carries none.
- **The words are subheadings, not tabs.** A click scrolls the page to that section on the signature
  ease and the section's own pills take the left slot. The bar sticks to the top of the sheet while
  the page scrolls (a hairline and the resting shadow say so), and scroll-spy lights the word and
  swaps the pills on a manual scroll too.
- **The pills are filters inside their section.** The playbook groups `All · Foundations · Content ·
  Campaigns` on one, the thread statuses `All · needs you · in progress · settled` on the other, the
  Home ledger's chips and one `threadStatus` derivation with the bell.
- **The hand-off (docs/33 §7).** The scroll takes 420 ms on `--ease`, the hand-off duration, and the
  section's first group head lands under the bar. The old chips leave in 130 ms (`--dur-exit`), the
  new ones enter with `nm-rise`, staggered 32 ms after a 150 ms lead, so they land with the scroll.
  The lit word is colour only, 150 ms. The stuck edge fades in over 120 ms. Reduced motion jumps.
- **The project pill keeps scoping the whole screen.** Unscoped, the desk is a strip of tiles (logo ·
  name · the score dial or the door the project still needs · facts · connector marks). Scoped, the
  desk is one line, and the room's Connections list stays under it, unchanged.
- **The catalog rows keep today's anatomy** plus a glyph. The trailing slot rests as a fact (the
  newest score with its age, or the armed cadence, with the project the scope does not say, or
  *never run*). The verb shows on hover or focus: `Run for… ›` unscoped, opening today's picker, and
  `Run ›` when a project is picked. Pills and rows still draft a message, never a command.
- **The threads keep the ledger's row**, `Needs you` pinned first under All, then the day groups,
  `project · #marketing` in the trailing slot only when unscoped, `Show n more` past the cap.

## 5. Left as is, on purpose

- The scoped Connections section stays under the desk line. The boards drew the marks as doors
  (the composer foot's rule) and the section retired. A connect popover on the tile is a follow-up.
- `setupProgressLabel` still says `step 1 of 4 — Product` with an em dash (shared copy, its own PR).
- The harness cannot capture the hover verb: programmatic focus does not trigger `:focus-visible`
  in Chromium, so `mkos7` shows the row focused with its fact. The verb is CSS on `:hover` and
  `:focus-visible` only.
- The four failures in `src/main/sync/ipc/foreground.test.ts` fail the same way on a clean checkout
  of `main` on this machine (`Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'registerRoomIpc')`). This
  branch touches no main-process code.

## 6. Traps this build met

- **`lint-ratchet.mjs --update` wiped every burn-down entry** in this worktree and reported
  "0 burn-down entries". Reverted. Do not run the update from a worktree under `.claude/` without
  checking the diff. Explicit `eslint <files>` and `eslint .` do lint here (0 errors).
- **A fresh worktree lacks the linked workspace packages** (`@neuramesh/fonts`,
  `@neuramesh/relay-client`): `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` first, or the preview build and the
  typecheck fail on imports the branch never touched.
- **`vite` is not a direct dependency of the desktop package**, so `pnpm exec vite` fails there.
  The preview harness builds with `node node_modules/.pnpm/vite@*/node_modules/vite/bin/vite.js
  build --config apps/desktop/vite.preview.config.mts` from the root, then
  `NM_SHOOT_INDEX=out/preview/index.html … pnpm exec electron scripts/shoot.cjs` from apps/desktop.
- **The harness's Acme project is named `v0.9 release`**: a shot that picks it by `/acme/` lands on
  Acme Yoga, which has no marketing room.
