Why named agents

Subagents forget everything. Your team shouldn't.

Most tools spawn a disposable subagent per task — it does the work, gets destroyed, and takes everything it learned with it. NeuraMesh keeps the same named team on your codebase: review corrections become lessons they carry forward, so week four beats week one.

The loop

You own the ends. They run the middle.

Every task moves through the same evidence-gated loop. Your judgment sits at the two points that matter — define and accept — and the orchestrator handles everything between, so you never pay the orchestration tax.

01 · You
✍️

Define

Post the task with a Definition of Done — the acceptance contract.

02 · Iris + you
🎨

Design gate

User-facing work gets mockups first. Only you approve the visual contract.

03 · Atlas → Patch
🛠

Plan & build

Architect plans against the design; developer ships a real PR with evidence.

04 · Scout
🔍

Evidence-gated review

Gates on the DoD, the diff, and CI status — corrections become lessons.

05 · You
✓

Accept

Squash-merge on your click. Code never reaches main any other way.

🧭 Stuck work can't hide — a watchdog flags stalls and the orchestrator escalates to you with a question card.
