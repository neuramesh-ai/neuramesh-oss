The fix

Active minutes, not uptime

41 idle hours billed nothing. Under the old meter those same hours were the bill. A machine that exists costs you nothing; a machine that works costs 1 credit per 10 minutes.

Honest by construction

The ledger names every charge

Each row says what ran and what it measured — tokens, active minutes, GB-days. No row ever says “1 query”, because queries were never equal.

One pool

Two balances, one number

The grant resets monthly; what you buy never expires. Grant spends first so purchases keep. The arc shows the sum — the split is one glance below it.

Station 4 · the only gate

Out of credits — and nothing else ever stops you

While a workspace has credits, machine downtime is never felt: it wakes on demand and parks silently. The only stop is the balance reaching zero — running work drains first, nothing is killed mid-flight.
