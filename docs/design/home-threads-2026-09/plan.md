## 1. The ask

George (2026-09-11): "make the homescreen show recent threads similar to the threads search or home
notifications with filters, with the composer at the top; right now it seems we have too much unused
space on the new chat screen, and users have to click on notifications to see items needing attention."

**Litmus:** faster (what needs you is on the landing, no click) · safer (one derivation feeds the bell,
the rail, ⌘Y and Home, so they cannot disagree) · delightful (the landing answers its first question
before you ask it). Yes.

## 2. The reversal, said out loud

Home has moved before: v0.56 put the composer at the bottom of a landing, v0.94 made Home the briefing
with no composer, and 2026-08-16 reduced Home to a greeting and the composer because Recent lived in
the rail and ⌘Y, and the queue in the bell. docs/35 §3.3 also said the session list is "never a filtered
view". This round reverses both on purpose, and both docs carry the amendment. The rule that survives
underneath is the one that matters: ONE derivation of the rows and their status.

## 3. What was decided

- **Two groups, never the day buckets.** The asks pinned under `NEEDS YOU · n`, then `RECENT`. The room
  list keeps its day buckets; Home does not need them.
- **The composer moves to the top and widens to the reading column** (860px, the thread composer's
  width), so the composer and the list share one column. The foot's pills and four marks fit with room.
- **The ⌘Y overlay's own filter chips**, with counts over the project-narrowed set, and a project menu.
  The search well is a **door to ⌘Y**: the overlay keeps the only search field in the product.
- **Capped at 40 recent rows**, and `Show n more` lifts the cap in steps of 40 (the rail's idiom, George
  2026-09-11: "sounds good"), with `Search every conversation ⌘Y` as the floor. The asks are never capped.
  A new narrowing starts at the first step again.
- **A workspace with nothing to list keeps the centred stage.** The watermark hides under a list.
- **The bell stays.** It counts and opens from every other screen. Home is the destination.

## 5. Not in this round

- **The history watch's ceiling, filed as its own item.** Home pages the rows it has, but the rows it
  has are capped upstream: the workspace history watch (`nm:watch-history-all`, and the browser lane's
  `HISTORY_ALL_SQL`) takes the newest 400 threads, and `historyRows` slices to 200. A workspace past
  that is blind to its older threads on Home, in the rail and in ⌘Y, search included. The fix is a
  keyset cursor on `updated_at` so ⌘Y and Home's `Show more` can pull older pages from local SQLite on
  demand, with the live watch kept on the first page only. Nothing here touches sync rules: PowerSync
  already replicates every thread. Own round, because a live watch over older pages needs care.

- The phone's Home already lists threads with a status filter; it is unchanged.
- The ScopePill keeps its own menu recipe; Home only restyles the pill to the rail's quiet label.
- The two real app strings the mockup rewrote ("Answering happens in the thread", "awaiting your
  reply") still stand in the bell; they are a copy pass, not this round.

## 6. Harness notes

- George's first pass on the live harness (2026-09-11): the filter row sat 8px under the composer and
  indented 14px, because a bare `.ledgerfilt` rule lost the cascade to `.histovlfilter`, declared later
  in tokens.css with the same specificity. The compound `.histovlfilter.ledgerfilt` wins, at 32px. And
  every composer menu opened UPWARD off the top of the view, because the composer family anchors its
  popovers `bottom: calc(100% + n)` for a composer at the bottom of a sheet. On the ledgered Home they
  open downward (docs/33 §8 item 5), and the machine chip's height cap measures that side.
- George's second pass (same day): the project menu's rows wore the filter chips' skin (mono, boxed),
  because `.histovlfilter button` styled every button in the row, the ScopePill's popover rows included.
  The chip skin is scoped to DIRECT children now (`.histovlfilter > button`). The @ and / pickers opened
  under the whole box, far from the line that named them: they live inside the input's grow wrapper now,
  which the ledgered Home positions so they hang from the input (elsewhere it stays unpositioned and they
  anchor to the box as before). The Home placeholder is one plain sentence pair.

- The settle control reveals on `:focus-within` as well as hover, so a shot focuses the row's button.
- `.histovlfilter button` styles every button in the row, the ScopePill's and the search door's
  included; the ledger's own rules sit later with higher specificity.
