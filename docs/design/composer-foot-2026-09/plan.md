## 1. The ask

George (2026-09-10): show the Home composer's suggestion pills in an extended layer of the composer
itself, the way Claude Desktop's footer strip sits inside its composer, and put the supported
connectors' icons at the right of that strip, so a click sets up a connector that is not connected.

**Litmus:** faster (the three asks and every connector are one click from the landing, and a
connect no longer means leaving Home for a room's Workbench) · safer (one derivation of
"connected" behind two surfaces, one connect flow per provider) · delightful (the composer says
what you can ask and what the team can reach, in one calm object). Yes.

## 2. What was decided

- **Direction A, "the foot".** A third zone on the composer card: the chint cap's material mirrored
  at the bottom (full-bleed, one hairline, the well wash, the corners inherited). One card, one
  border, one focus ring. The B tray (a well hung under the card) costs a second edge and stops the
  focus ring at the card. The C live read (rex reads the room and replaces the door pill) is more
  scope and stays parked.
- **Four marks plus ⋯, not eight.** The stage column is 640px, the box's inside 612px. The three
  pills take about 450px. Eight 24px targets need about 206px and do not fit on one line. Four do.
  The foot shows X · LinkedIn · Instagram · TikTok in a FIXED order (never sorted by state) and
  PostHog · Meta Ads · TikTok Ads · image generation live behind ⋯.
- **Marks are monochrome simplified geometry**, the provider-marks precedent in `ui/icons.tsx`,
  never a colour logo and never a text glyph. Not connected = `--dim`. Connected = `--body` ink and a
  5px `--green` dot. A dead grant (`reauth_required` / `revoked` with a handle) = the `--warn` dot
  the bell already wears, and the verb is Reconnect.
- **A mark is a door.** Not connected opens the connect step in the composer's own popover recipe
  (the machine chip's `.cprojpop.cmachpop`, hung from the cluster's right edge). Connected shows the
  account and Disconnect (the list's two-step "Sure?"). ⋯ lists every connector as icon · name ·
  state, the machine-chip row idiom, and a row opens that connector's face with a back arrow.
- **A draft in the box hides the pills, keeps the marks, and the foot keeps its height** so nothing
  jumps under the first keystroke (the settle control's reserved-seat rule, docs/33 §4).
- **Copy follows CLAUDE.md #11.** The placeholder lost its em dash ("Describe the work, or just
  ask."). The connect copy moved to active voice with an actor ("neuramesh publishes a post only
  after you approve it").

## 3. What was built

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| The registry + the ONE derivation of connected (`CONNECTORS`, `FOOT_MARKS`, `connectorStates`, `imageCredOf`), tested | `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/settings/connectors.ts` · `connectors.test.ts` (7 cases) |

| ONE connect step per provider (OAuth hand-off · key verify-then-save · the image key form) + `disconnectConnector`; extracted from the Connections list | `settings/ConnectPanel.tsx` |

| The foot's marks, the popover and its three faces | `composer/ConnectorMarks.tsx` |
| The foot itself, inside the box; `.stagesugg` under the box retired | `views/NewChatStage.tsx` · `tokens.css` (`.cfoot*`, `.conn*`) |
| The Connections list, now on the shared derivation, the shared step and the SVG marks | `settings/ConnectionsList.tsx` (281 → 60 lines; the old exports re-exported) |
| The design system entry | `docs/33-design-system.md` §8 Composer ▸ the foot |

Zero schema, zero PowerSync, zero server change. The browser client keeps MCP keys unwired, so
every read in the hook is optional and a failed read keeps the last truth.

## 4. Scope the round did NOT take

- The phone's `new.tsx` keeps its three static suggestions under the box.
- The thread composer and the launcher wear no foot.
- A key connector armed from Home arms the TARGET room's flag, the same write the room's own list
  makes. A room that is not a marketing room can hold the flag; nothing reads it there.
- The Connections list's refactor is covered by the typecheck, the derivation test and the full
  desktop suite (1295 pass). It was not re-shot on the harness: the list mounts only in a scoped
  Marketing OS or a marketing room's Workbench, which the harness does not drive from a query.

## 5. Harness notes

- A shot that clicks a mark by its `aria-label` depends on the fixture's state: X is connected in
  the fixture, so the connect shot targets Instagram.
- A tooltip inside an `overflow-y: auto` popover is absolute-positioned but still scrollable
  overflow; a centred tip on the last column grew the list a horizontal scrollbar. The state tips
  now anchor to the popover's right edge and the popover hides x-overflow.
