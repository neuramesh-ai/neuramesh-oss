## Problem Statement

George, on the round-2 mockups: "i don't like the 'your people, your agents, your projects', its too
bland; also the homepage copy needs to be rethought." The triad was locked on 2026-07-23 as a setup
line with a punchline under it ("One org. One memory. One set of rules they can't break."). The
cloud-first round kept the setup and dropped the punchline, so the hero became three nouns and no
claim.

The rethink is bigger than a headline. George is repositioning the product around three pillars:

1. Fast brains. Mostly flash models, guided by the harness, so routines and tasks turn around
   faster and cheaper. Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite is already the default cloud brain for the remote
   runner. Brand: the fast alternative.
2. Outcomes, not developer tools. End-to-end outcomes for the AI founder or lead. Individual tasks
   taken end to end, fast and efficient.
3. Vetted experts. Extend the catalog with tested expert agents anyone can hire with a single
   command. They run on the user's own workspace and finish tasks.

## Demand Evidence

## Target User and Narrowest Wedge

The person, in George's words: the AI founder or lead. Revised in this session: "this person
doesn't have to be alone, thats why neuramesh supports a team plan; they can invite their team to
collaborate on the workspace." So the page speaks to the founder or lead first, and the team is the

The wedge stays the first agent reply in the browser, in one session, with no install and no key.
The fast claim sharpens it: the first result arrives in minutes, on the starter brain.

## Positions taken in the session

- The fast alternative is a moat only as fast to an accepted outcome. Speed from a model choice
  is not a moat: any harness can flip to a flash model in an afternoon. The defensible version is
  the loop that lets a cheap model finish real work: the plan gate where the work is big, a
  reviewer that is a different agent, evidence before submit, lessons that persist.
- This reverses the July fast-pack ruling of no mini or lite working seats. The reversal stands on
  the bench, not on taste.
- The product is not fast today. A trivial plan measured about four minutes, the cold-start floor
  about 2.75 minutes, and the first cloud stamp 106 seconds. Those are launch blockers for this
  page.
- "End to end" collides with two human gates. The page says end to end only about work that runs
  with no gate, and names the two gates as gates.
- "Marketplace" is not said this round. A marketplace with no sellers and no buyers is the
  platform-vision pattern. The page says vetted experts you hire with one command, and the first
  catalog is first-party.

EUREKA, named and logged: everyone assumes agent quality comes from the model. NeuraMesh's bet is
that it comes from the loop, which lets a flash model finish accepted work at a fraction of the
time and cost.

## Approaches Considered (the hero)

### Approach A: From ask to done in minutes (chosen)
Kicker: the fast way to run a company with agents. Headline: from ask to done in minutes. The
claim and the number in one plain line, the category in the kicker. Con: any vendor can also say
minutes, so the clock has to be real.

### Approach B: Minutes, not sessions
Names the enemy in three words. Rejected this round: "sessions" is insider vocabulary for a
marketer or an operator.

### Approach C: The fast way to run a company with agents
The category claim as the headline. Rejected as the headline, kept as the kicker: the longest
line, and "with agents" is the crowded phrase.

## Launch gates for this page (premise 3)

The headline is the fast claim, not only the stamp. So the gates hold the headline, the "in
minutes" lines in section 01, and the catalog, not only the number:

1. Ask-to-approve for a content task on the starter brain, measured on ten real asks: median under
   three minutes. Gates the headline, the hero clock, and every "in minutes" line.
2. Cold start: a stranger's first reply under sixty seconds, warm capacity or a faster stamp. Gates
   "fast by default".
3. The bench (`packages/bench`, /model-benchmarks): the Starter model's acceptance rate on content,
   research, and routine tasks within ten points of the frontier pack. Gates section 01.
4. The catalog: at least five first-party experts, each with a measured turnaround and a bench
   score, and a hire command that exists in the product. Gates section 02.

If the page must go live before gate 1 holds, the fallback hero is D2's option B: headline
"Finished work, approved by you.", the same kicker, the same lead without "on fast models", and no
clock. The SAMPLE tag is a mockup device for this canvas, never a shipped element.
